# SOL Editor v0.4.0 — engine contract consumer

This compatibility release follows SOL Engine v0.4.0 without restarting parked
story or production-level work.

## Contract consumption

- bundle contract 2 and SOLPACK schema 2;
- wadpack contract 3 with 18 active resources across 20 logical slots;
- PreciseCrosshair v1.5.0 active at slot 19;
- retired slot 11 and reserved slot 20 never materialized or mounted;
- SOL runtime/content at slots 21/22;
- SOLDEFAULTS contract 1 remains engine-owned;
- story contract 1 remains intact.

The editor's wadpack/bundle entry points delegate to the sibling engine.
`sol-project/wadpack.json` is retained only as a deprecation pointer.

## E1M1 systems testbed

The deterministic generated E1M1 regression fixture is presented as `TESTMAP`.
It now contains 15 room themes and 47 connected sectors while remaining
monster-free. It retains all six major weapon pickups and multiplayer starts,
and expands stock prop coverage to 126 decorations, barrels, torches, and
corpses for ReLite, Universal Ambience, resource-ordering, renderer, and editor
playtests.

TESTMAP contains two reciprocal linked-portal demonstrations under geometry
contract 1.

The illusion pair uses linedef IDs 9001 and 9002. The local illusion chamber is
entered through an ordinary side connection from the existing route. Its portal
threshold is on another wall, so the player can enter normally, turn away from
the threshold, and back into it. The remote chamber exits through a different
side connection. Looking back after leaving the remote chamber therefore does
not place the original physical entrance directly behind the portal. The effect
is produced by authored layout and occlusion; no player-direction portal rule is
required.

The portal-lab pair uses linedef IDs 9011 and 9012. It remains intentionally
visible so linked-portal rendering can be inspected directly, but it is moved
into a dedicated decorated branch of TESTMAP. Access is through one initially
closed tagged sector opened by a player-use `Door_Open` line. This keeps the raw
portal demonstration available for engine testing without leaving an undecorated
portal threshold in the main route.

The generator, validator, tracked statistics, and layout are versioned together
under testbed contract 1. Validation requires both reciprocal portal pairs, the
Portal Lab door contract, zero monsters, all six weapon pickups, and the exact
tracked generated outputs.

## Content package

`tools/sol-build.sh` produces:

```text
build/sol/sol-e1m1-v0.4.0.pk3
```

The engine-owned final builder places that content at slot 22 after the
SOL-owned runtime at slot 21.

## Local-only resource boundary

The complete `sol.pk3` remains a local development artifact. Third-party source
archives and normalized runtime inputs remain under the shared local `vend`
tree and are never committed merely because they are part of the canonical
local test stack.
