# SOL Editor v0.4.0 — engine contract consumer

This compatibility release follows SOL Engine v0.4.0 without restarting parked
story or production-level work.

## Contract consumption

- bundle contract 2 and SOLPACK schema 2;
- wadpack contract 3 with 18 active resources across 20 logical slots;
- PreciseCrosshair v1.5.0 active at slot 19;
- retired slot 11 and reserved slot 20 never materialized or mounted;
- SOL runtime/content at slots 21/22;
- SOLDEFAULTS contract 1 remains engine-owned;
- story contract 1 remains intact.

The editor's wadpack/bundle entry points now delegate to the sibling engine.
`sol-project/wadpack.json` is retained only as a deprecation pointer.

## E1M1 systems testbed

The deterministic generated E1M1 regression fixture has thirteen room themes
and 42 connected sectors. It uses stock Doom placeholder materials, varied
lighting/heights, no monsters, all six major weapon pickups, multiplayer starts,
supplies, and 120 stock decorations/barrels/torches/corpses. The phase approach
and local dead-end corridor are intentionally clear of blocking scenery, while
the rest of the fixture remains densely dressed. It also distinguishes
the stateful teleport-style phase source 9001 and local-only destination anchor
9002 from the independent reciprocal 9011/9012 linked-portal laboratory. This gives
ReLite, Universal Ambience, resource ordering, renderer, and editor playtests a
substantially broader fixture without treating E1M1 as production content.

The generator, validator, tracked stats, and layout are versioned together under
testbed contract 1.

## Content package

`tools/sol-build.sh` now produces:

```text
build/sol/sol-e1m1-v0.4.0.pk3
```

The engine-owned final builder places that content at slot 22 after the
SOL-owned runtime at slot 21.

## Local-only resource boundary

The complete `sol.pk3` remains a local development artifact. Third-party source
archives and normalized runtime inputs remain under the shared local `vend`
tree and are never committed merely because they are part of the canonical
local test stack.
